## Shared form fields — English

### Project name

Sourcebrief

### Tagline

Market claims change. Keep the evidence.

### One-sentence description

An evidence desk for Solana market research: inspect a prediction market's resolution rules or a launch pool's on-chain configuration, preserve a checkpoint, compare what changed, and export a dated brief.

### Short description

Sourcebrief turns Panta prediction-market responses and Solami mainnet reads of Meteora DBC pools into reviewable evidence briefs. It preserves rules, account context, exact amounts and uncertainty, then lets a researcher save a checkpoint, compare supported facts and export Markdown or JSON. It does not trade or predict outcomes.

### Colosseum project description

A market headline can outlive the evidence behind it. A prediction market's price is not its resolution rule; a launch curve reaching its threshold is not the same as migration being executed. Sourcebrief is an evidence desk designed for the researcher or community editor who needs to explain those distinctions before publishing a market note.

The workflow is the same for both kinds of market: inspect the source, preserve a checkpoint, review a later observation, and carry the evidence into a dated Markdown or JSON brief. The two evidence types stay separate. Sourcebrief does not imply that a prediction market and an unrelated token launch have a causal or trading relationship.

For prediction markets, authenticated read-only Panta catalogue and detail requests supply resolution wording, source identifiers, provider prices and separately labelled volume fields. The application distinguishes missing values from zero, resolved values from indicative prices, and actual tracked changes from a new request timestamp. Provider-supplied source identifiers are not presented as independently authenticated sources.

For token launches, Solami is the mainnet data path. Sourcebrief reads a Meteora DBC pool, discovers its configuration and mints, then fetches the pool, configuration, both mint accounts and Solana Clock together. It verifies program ownership, account layouts, referenced addresses and the derived pool address. The official Meteora SDK decodes the curve, fees, activation and migration state, and computes a local exact-input scenario where supported. Amounts use integer arithmetic; each final account has a content hash and a shared RPC context slot.

The result is an inspectable research artifact instead of an unexplained score. A completed curve is distinguished from a migrated pool. Transfer-hook and Token-2022 behavior that the calculator does not simulate produces an explicit abstention. Configured liquidity shares are not represented as verified holdings in the destination pool. No wallet, transaction signing or broadcast is required.

Real Panta production reads and real Solami mainnet reads have been verified. The reproducible recorded samples retain their original observation times and are labelled recorded; they are not a substitute for the live-mainnet demonstration. The repository documents setup with the operator's own private provider keys. No external users, revenue, trading volume, return improvement or forecast accuracy have been measured.

### What is different

Sourcebrief concentrates on what a market explanation can actually support. A chart or account explorer exposes individual values; this workflow ties the relevant source wording or program configuration to an observation time, preserves it, distinguishes meaningful changes from transport metadata, and exports its limits with the facts. The contribution is the complete review trail, not a claim that account explorers or research notebooks did not exist before it.

### Technology and Solana integration

Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript; Node.js; authenticated Panta catalogue/detail API; Solami read-only mainnet RPC; and the official Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve SDK 1.5.13. The launch service verifies the full mainnet genesis hash before reading DBC accounts. It uses a final five-account getMultipleAccounts response for coherent evidence, fixed-length account decoding, SDK pool-address derivation and exact BN/BigInt calculations. There is no custom Solana program, signed transaction, trading agent or LLM generating the market facts.

### Audience and validation

The initial intended users are community editors, launchpad reviewers and researchers preparing a market explanation or reviewing a configuration change. These are proposed users, not measured customers. The next validation is a small opt-in pilot: ask a participant to produce one brief and review a known change, then record completion, misunderstandings and feedback. A synthetic test case will remain explicitly synthetic; it will not be reported as a live market event or a human outcome.

Initial distribution would focus on practical example briefs and documentation for Solana research communities and launchpad builders, followed by opt-in pilot users. No outreach, partnership or adoption is claimed. Before proposing paid hosting, we would validate demand, provider commercial terms, operating costs and durable free-versus-paid access. It does more than display a token price: it explains the current curve boundaries, quote reserve versus migration threshold, activation, fee behavior and configured post-migration liquidity split, then saves the evidence for later comparison and export.

The Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve SDK 1.5.13 is central to the implementation. Transfer-hook pools can be inspected, but their external hook execution is not simulated; Token-2022 calculation also abstains in this version.

The working prototype has been tested against real third-party mainnet pools, including active, migrated and transfer-hook cases. It neither creates those pools nor claims their users or volume.
