# Sourcebrief brand

Status: agent-selected under Jhona's request for autonomous polished work.

Direction: a contemporary research desk and carefully typeset evidence sheet. Warm ivory page (#f5f3ec), paper surface (#fffef9), almost-black ink (#20241f), muted ink (#596154), fine olive-grey rules (#c8cbbf), ultramarine emphasis (#263db7), pale blue highlight (#e9edff), amber attention (#81510d on #fff0d3). Colors are CSS tokens. No gradient blobs, arbitrary badges, fake metrics, logo clouds or decorative charts.

Typography: Georgia for short expressive headings; Segoe UI/system sans for controls and prose; Consolas/system monospace for timestamps and field identifiers. Body minimum 15px, explanatory text minimum 13px with AA contrast. Dates/prices tabular. Large heading bounded and responsive, not a page-covering billboard.

Layout: precise compact masthead, a short orientation, searchable catalogue rail, substantial evidence sheet. Responsive single column on mobile. Deliberate numbered sections and thin rules give identity. A simple custom inline SVG source/document symbol is sufficient; no external images/fonts required.

Motion: only functional 150–200ms transitions and subtle entry; reduced-motion disables nonessential effects. Keyboard-visible focus, 44px controls, loading/empty/error/offline states. All actions actually work.

Voice: calm, specific, transparent. Say 'Source identifier supplied', not 'Verified'. Say 'Indicative unit price', not 'Chance of winning'. Say 'Captured at', not 'Live' for a saved record. Avoid marketing promises and technical implementation copy in the main task flow.
