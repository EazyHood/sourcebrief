Read the pitch transcript

A market claim travels. Does its evidence? A price leaves out resolution rules. A progress bar does not prove migration. Missing context follows the claim into a report.

Sourcebrief is a solo project by EazyHood, built with extensive OpenAI Codex assistance to make research briefs easier to check. Inspect the source. Save a checkpoint. Compare a later observation. Export a dated brief, without losing the original evidence.

Panta supplies the catalogue and market details: resolution wording, source identifiers, indicated prices and missing information.

Solami reads the DBC pool, configuration, both token mints and Clock together on mainnet. The brief keeps their shared context slot and account hashes. An actual application capture is shown at native resolution as a static illustration; the separate demonstration shows application use.

Meteora’s official SDK interprets curves, fees and migration. It calculates a local scenario where supported. No wallet, signature or executed transaction. For migrated pools or external transfer hooks, the calculator explains why it abstains instead of inventing an output.

Two evidence types, one editorial workflow. No invented link between an event and a token. Export observations, calculations and limits.

No measured external traction or revenue yet. Next: an opt-in reviewer pilot. A shared team workspace is proposed, not built. Sourcebrief. Market claims change. Keep the evidence. Explore the open-source code and the live research desk.

Server-side Solana mainnet reads. A DBC pool and its related accounts share a context slot, with hashes carried into the evidence.
Meteora DBC
Official SDK interpretation and local quote calculations, with clear abstention when migration or an external transfer hook makes the scenario unsuitable.

These are research tools. A source identifier is not independent verification of the source’s claim. A local scenario is not an executed transaction, a safety assessment or an investment recommendation. Third-party pool activity is not Sourcebrief user traction.
